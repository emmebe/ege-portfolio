About Me

I’m Emma Grace Ebeling (EGE)—a designer with dual BAs in Games and Interactive Media and Information Science, plus a minor in Korean Language and Culture (including study abroad at Yonsei University, Seoul). I build digital experiences where research, accessibility, and storytelling meet clear interface craft.

This portfolio collects selected work: game design documentation, UI/UX prototypes, live web builds, user research, and multimedia design. Each project includes process notes so you can see how I think, not only what I shipped. I’m especially interested in human-centered products—thoughtful flows, legible systems, and design decisions grounded in real user needs.
