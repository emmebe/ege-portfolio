Why this work matters

Commerce flows require clarity around catalog browsing, item detail, and checkout mental models. Medium fidelity is ideal for aligning structure before pixel polish.

Process

  • Mapped core shopping paths and content types (browse, detail, cart, account).
  • Established layout rhythm and component placeholders without final brand assets.
  • Reviewed flows for missing states (empty cart, errors, loading) at a structural level.

Outcomes & value

  • Validates information architecture early with less design churn later.
  • Demonstrates systems thinking for multi-screen retail experiences.
  • Bridge artifact between lo-fi discovery and hi-fi visual design.
