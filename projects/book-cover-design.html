Book and Magazine Covers

Cover compositions that communicate genre, tone, and audience at a glance.

Open full image
Visual DesignBrandingComposition

Why this work matters

Covers are conversion design: hierarchy, metaphor, and restraint determine whether someone picks up the work. Similar principles apply to app store assets and campaign hero images.

Process

  • Researched genre conventions, then chose when to align versus subvert them.
  • Iterated thumbnail-scale compositions to ensure clarity at small sizes.
  • Balanced imagery, title treatment, and author or series information.

Outcomes & value

  • Demonstrates art direction and compositional decision-making.
  • Shows empathy for audience expectations without being generic.
  • Transferable to marketing design and product launch visuals.
