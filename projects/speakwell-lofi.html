MobileLo-FiWireframes

Why this work matters

Lo-fi wireframes prove you can frame learning goals, lesson structure, and navigation before investing in visual design—critical for ed-tech and internal training products.

Process

  • Identified learner tasks: onboarding, modules, practice, and progress tracking.
  • Sketched screen-to-screen flows focusing on content hierarchy, not color or branding.
  • Iterated layout options for dense instructional content on small screens.

Outcomes & value

  • Fast feedback cycles with instructors or stakeholders on structure.
  • Reduces rework when hi-fi UI is applied on a validated skeleton.
  • Shows comfort working from problem → flow → fidelity escalation.
