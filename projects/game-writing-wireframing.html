Narrative Design Wireframing Information Architecture

Why this work matters

Narrative designers are expected to plan how writing surfaces in UI—not only what characters say. Flow diagrams show employers you can structure branching dialogue, barks, and quest text before engineering implements localization pipelines or CMS tools.

Process

  • Identified player touchpoints where text appears (menus, HUD, cutscenes, interactables).
  • Sketched state flows for branching conversations and failure/retry paths.
  • Aligned narrative beats with UI containers so writers and interface designers share a blueprint.

Outcomes & value

  • Surfaces scope early (how many strings, variants, and triggers the game needs).
  • Reduces rework when narrative and UX are designed in parallel.
  • Demonstrates systems thinking applied to story delivery—not just prose samples.
