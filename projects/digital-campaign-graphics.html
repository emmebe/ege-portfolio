Why this work matters

Campaign assets must communicate one idea instantly. Employers value designers who understand format specs, safe zones, and message hierarchy for noisy feeds.

Process

  • Defined a single primary message and CTA per asset.
  • Adapted layouts across aspect ratios while keeping brand elements consistent.
  • Tested legibility on mobile previews and adjusted contrast accordingly.

Outcomes & value

  • Shows speed and clarity in marketing contexts.
  • Demonstrates brand consistency across variants.
  • Pairs well with information design and UX writing collaboration.
