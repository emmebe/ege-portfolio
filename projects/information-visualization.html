Information Visualization

Charts and diagrams that make quantitative stories understandable at a glance.

Open full image
Data VizInformation DesignAccessibility

Why this work matters

Data visualization is UX for numbers: encoding, labeling, and color choices either build trust or mislead. Teams need designers who pick the right chart for the question.

Process

  • Started from the question the audience needs answered, not the chart type that looks interesting.
  • Applied restrained color palettes and direct labeling to reduce legend hunting.
  • Checked color contrast and whether categories are distinguishable for common color-vision needs.

Outcomes & value

  • Supports clearer stakeholder decisions in research and product reviews.
  • Demonstrates rigor alongside creativity in visual work.
  • Foundation for dashboard design and in-product analytics views.
