BrandingPrintArt Direction

Why this work matters

Brand-forward print work shows you can own a cohesive visual language—critical for design systems, marketing sites, and product identity.

Process

  • Established mood boards and typographic voice before final composition.
  • Aligned photography, color, and type to a single narrative hook.
  • Refined details (kerning, bleed, safe areas) for production readiness.

Outcomes & value

  • Demonstrates end-to-end visual ownership from concept to artifact.
  • Builds credibility for cross-media brand projects.
  • Useful when teams need one designer to span marketing and product surfaces.
