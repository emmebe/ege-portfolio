MyndBody Wellness Tracker

Click-through mobile prototype for daily logging, progress views, and wellness habit loops.

Open in Figma
MobileLo-FiPrototype

Why this work matters

Health apps must make logging and reflection feel lightweight. An interactive lo-fi prototype tests whether the flow feels motivating before visual branding.

Process

  • Outlined daily tracking loops and where users enter data versus review insights.
  • Linked screens in Figma proto mode for click-through testing.
  • Adjusted steps per screen based on perceived friction in informal walkthroughs.

Outcomes & value

  • Surfaces flow length and tap cost early—key for habit-forming products.
  • Demonstrates prototype literacy beyond static wireframes.
  • Foundation for hi-fi visual system and engineering estimates.
