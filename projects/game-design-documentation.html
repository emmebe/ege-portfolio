Game Design Documentation Systems Design

Why this work matters

Strong game pitches translate creative vision into something programmers, artists, and producers can schedule against. Employers look for designers who can define scope, articulate player goals, and document mechanics before production spend begins.

Process

  • Defined core loop, player fantasy, and design pillars to anchor feature decisions.
  • Mapped mechanics to intended emotional beats and session length targets.
  • Structured the document for cross-discipline handoff (systems, UX, narrative hooks).

Outcomes & value

  • Reduces ambiguity in early production by giving the team a shared source of truth.
  • Demonstrates ability to think in systems—not only visuals or story beats.
  • Supports iteration: constraints and success metrics are explicit enough to revisit after playtests.
