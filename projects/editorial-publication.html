Editorial and Publication Layouts

Print-inspired editorial grid for long-form readability and visual hierarchy.

Open full image
EditorialInDesignTypography

Why this work matters

Editorial work proves command of grids, typographic systems, and pacing—skills that transfer to dashboards, marketing pages, and documentation.

Process

  • Selected type pairings and column structure for scan-friendly long-form layout.
  • Used contrast, rules, and negative space to guide reading order.
  • Prepared assets at print-appropriate resolution and margins.

Outcomes & value

  • Shows attention to micro-typography and macro layout simultaneously.
  • Demonstrates ability to design for real production constraints.
  • Signals strong foundation for any content-heavy interface.
