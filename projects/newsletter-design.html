E-Info Design (Newsletters)

Digital newsletter layout balancing scanability, brand voice, and campaign goals.

Open full image
Information DesignVisual CommunicationCampaign

Why this work matters

Newsletters are information products: hierarchy, chunking, and calls-to-action matter as much as aesthetics. Employers in comms and UX-adjacent roles look for clarity under density.

Process

  • Established a typographic scale and grid for consistent scanning on desktop and mobile email clients.
  • Prioritized headline, summary, and CTA placement based on reader attention patterns.
  • Applied brand color and imagery deliberately to support comprehension, not decoration alone.

Outcomes & value

  • Improves reader completion rates by reducing cognitive load.
  • Shows editorial judgment: what to emphasize, compress, or cut.
  • Transferable to onboarding emails, release notes, and in-product messaging.
