Menu Design (Print)

Print menu system organizing offerings, pricing, and brand atmosphere for hospitality contexts.

Open PDF in new tab
Print DesignLayoutBrand

Why this work matters

Menus are practical information architecture: categories, scanning paths, and emphasis drive revenue and comprehension. The same thinking applies to settings screens and checkout flows.

Process

  • Grouped items into scannable sections with consistent pricing alignment.
  • Used typography and spacing to reduce decision fatigue in dense lists.
  • Matched visual tone to venue brand while keeping type highly legible.

Outcomes & value

  • Shows real-world information hierarchy outside of software mockups.
  • Demonstrates print production awareness (margins, bleed, PDF handoff).
  • Highlights versatility across digital and physical touchpoints.
