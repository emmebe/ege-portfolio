User Research

Research synthesis connecting qualitative insights to quantitative patterns and recommendations.

Open PDF in new tab
User ResearchData AnalysisRecommendations

Why this work matters

Hiring managers want evidence that design decisions respond to real user behavior. A concise research report proves you can collect, analyze, and translate findings into action.

Process

  • Defined research questions tied to product or service goals before gathering data.
  • Synthesized patterns across responses and triangulated with simple quantitative views where useful.
  • Prioritized high-impact insights with clear implications for design direction.

Outcomes & value

  • Gives stakeholders a shared rationale for what to build or fix first.
  • Reduces risk of shipping features that solve the wrong problem.
  • Demonstrates communication skill: making complex data legible to non-researchers.
